# Off-Site SEO & Distribution Playbook (`docs/OFFSITE-SEO.md`)

The static site build (`build.py`) handles on-page technical SEO: clean semantic HTML, unique `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` tags, canonical URLs, JSON-LD structured data (`Organization`, `WebSite`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Course`, `FAQPage`, `Article`), internal linking across the 9-guide internship cluster, and `sitemap.xml`.

To rank for competitive student search queries (`equity research internship online India`, `stock market internship for beginners`, `finance internship with certificate for students`, `NISM Series VIII syllabus`), complete the following off-site steps that cannot be automated inside the repository:

## 6. Internship Portals (Check Platform Rules First)

1. Before listing the **Equity Research Internship Program** on third-party student portals (Internshala, Unstop, LinkedIn Jobs, etc.), **carefully read each platform's terms of service regarding training programs / certificate fees**.
2. Many job boards strictly prohibit listing training programs with certificate or enrolment fees under standard employment categories (which is also why we never use `JobPosting` schema on the site). On platforms like Unstop, use the dedicated **Workshops / Courses / Training** category rather than paid employment listings.
