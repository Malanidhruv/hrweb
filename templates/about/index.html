About the Platform

About Harion Research

Harion Research is a financial education and research platform based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. We build public market screening tools and train aspiring analysts to use them with discipline.

What we do

We focus on financial market education and applied research, combining analytical thinking, financial statement interpretation and structured frameworks. We believe the real value of market study lies in clarity, discipline and a structured approach to information—process over prediction, rules over emotion, and insight through data.

Our work spans two connected pillars:

  • The Market Analysis Learning Lab (Research Scanner): A public educational tool for studying NSE and BSE equity universes across price action, volume behaviour, market structure and reported financial ratios on delayed end-of-day data.
  • The Equity Research Internship Program: A structured online training program where students and aspiring analysts use our screening tools, examine annual reports, build a financial model, and write a complete, mentor-reviewed equity research report.
01 / MISSION

Mission

To bridge the gap between academic finance education and analytical industry standards through practical market tools, certification-aligned learning, and mentor-reviewed research internships.

02 / VISION

Vision

To serve as a credible, transparent financial education and research platform in India that helps students from diverse academic backgrounds build verifiable analytical skills.

Core principles

PRINCIPLE 01

Rules over emotion

Objective, repeatable frameworks that reduce cognitive bias and build consistency.

PRINCIPLE 02

Insight through data

Structured datasets and evidence-based reasoning from primary filings instead of opinions.

PRINCIPLE 03

Risk-first thinking

Disciplined research that keeps balance-sheet uncertainty and model limitations in view.
