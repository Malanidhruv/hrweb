About the Platform

About Harion Research

Harion Research is a financial education and research platform based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. We combine structured mentorship with purpose-built analytical software to make studying Indian equities, international equity markets, and the broader financial system accessible to serious learners.

What we do

We focus on financial market education and applied research, uniting primary financial statement analysis, quantitative screening tools, and human mentorship. We believe the real value of market study lies in clarity, discipline, and a structured approach to information—process over prediction, rules over emotion, and insight through data.

Today, our ecosystem connects public analytical software with guided analyst training:

  • The Market Analysis Learning Lab (Research Scanner): Purpose-built analytical software for studying stock universes across price action, volume behaviour, market structure, and reported financial ratios on delayed end-of-day data—starting with NSE and BSE equities as part of our broader research toolkit.
  • The Equity Research Internship Program: A structured online training program where students and aspiring analysts use our screening tools, examine corporate filings, build a financial model, and write a complete, mentor-reviewed equity research report.
01 / MISSION

Mission

To democratize financial market education through structured mentorship and purpose-built software tools—equipping learners to understand Indian equities, international equity markets, and the broader financial market ecosystem with analytical rigor.

02 / VISION

Vision

To build an open, trusted research and learning platform where any dedicated student—regardless of academic background—can develop institutional-grade analytical skills across domestic and global markets.

Our training is open to every committed learner. Certification fees paid by participants on completion help sustain the research tools, mentorship and day-to-day operations behind this mission.

Core principles

PRINCIPLE 01

Rules over emotion

Objective, repeatable frameworks that reduce cognitive bias and build consistency.

PRINCIPLE 02

Insight through data

Structured datasets and evidence-based reasoning from primary filings instead of opinions.

PRINCIPLE 03

Risk-first thinking

Disciplined research that keeps balance-sheet uncertainty and model limitations in view.
