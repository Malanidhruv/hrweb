01 / Program Overview

An equity research internship online in India built around real tools

Most finance courses stop at textbook definitions. If you are looking for an equity research internship online India that mirrors how institutional and buy-side analysts actually study companies, this training program places you directly inside our analytical workflow.

Instead of watching passive slide decks, you start by screening NSE and BSE stock universes inside the Harion Market Analysis Learning Lab (Research Scanner). You select a real listed Indian company, examine multi-year annual reports and financial filings, build a historical and valuation spreadsheet model, and write a complete educational equity research report under mentor review.

02 / The Process

The workflow

Every participant moves through five structured stages over {{PROGRAM_DURATION}}:

  1. STAGE 01 Screen Filter NSE & BSE universes in the Harion scanner using financial ratios and price/volume structure.
  2. STAGE 02 Model Spread 5 years of income statements, balance sheets, cash flows and key operating ratios.
  3. STAGE 03 Write Draft a structured company overview, sector analysis, valuation context and risk assessment.
  4. STAGE 04 Review Submit drafts for mentor critique on accounting logic, evidence quality and analytical clarity.
  5. STAGE 05 Publish Graduate with a finished educational research report ready for your analytical portfolio.

04 / Syllabus & 40 Classroom Lessons

Curriculum

The curriculum combines 40 self-paced lessons in the Harion Classroom with weekly live review sessions over {{PROGRAM_DURATION}}.

Module 1: Research Methodology & Stock Screening (Lessons 1–7)

How equity research analysts structure an inquiry, avoid confirmation bias, and shortlist companies across NSE and BSE universes using financial, sectoral and market-structure parameters in the Harion Research Scanner.

Module 2: Reading Annual Reports & Financial Statements (Lessons 8–16)

Line-by-line study of the income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement and Notes to Accounts. Evaluating revenue recognition, working-capital intensity, cash conversion (OCF/EBITDA), debt schedules and contingent liabilities.

Module 3: Sector & Competitive Landscape Analysis (Lessons 17–23)

Mapping industry structure, cyclicality, raw-material sensitivity, pricing power and capital allocation across peers. Comparing Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) and Return on Equity (ROE) drivers across an industry cohort.

Module 4: Financial Modelling & Valuation Logic (Lessons 24–31)

Building structured historical spreads, common-size statements, DuPont decompositions, and understanding how discounted cash flow (DCF) and relative valuation multiples (P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA) are interpreted alongside their underlying assumptions.

Module 5: Guided Market Structure Observation (Lessons 32–35)

Studying historical price and volume behaviour, relative strength and market regimes on delayed End-of-Day data (at least three months old for live case discussions, aligned with SEBI educational guidelines), with zero live speculative trading.

Module 6: Research Documentation & Capstone Report (Lessons 36–40)

Drafting company notes, structuring arguments clearly, presenting risks objectively, incorporating mentor revisions, and submitting your final educational equity research report on a listed Indian company.

06 / Feedback Loop

Mentor review process

Self-study often stalls because no one checks whether your assumptions hold up. During the program, mentors review your company selection, financial ratio spreads and draft report sections during weekly review sessions.

  • Evidence check: Every claim in your report must trace back to an annual report, exchange filing or verifiable dataset.
  • Accounting & ratio check: Reviewing how you calculate ROCE, free cash flow, working-capital days and peer multiples.
  • Editorial clarity: Ensuring your report presents balanced analysis and risk factors rather than promotional narratives.

10 / Program FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this an employment job or an educational training program?

The Harion Equity Research Internship Program is a structured online educational training program where you learn equity research methodology and complete a mentor-reviewed research report on a listed company. It is not an employment contract.

What are the duration and program fee?

The program duration is {{PROGRAM_DURATION}}. The fee structure is {{PROGRAM_PRICE}}. All 40 self-paced lessons in the Harion Classroom are accessible online, and the optional verified completion certificate is issued after mentor review of your final report.

Do I need prior experience in financial modelling or coding?

No prior experience is required. The curriculum begins with financial statement foundations and guides you step by step through using the Harion Research Scanner and building your company model in standard spreadsheets.

Can I complete the program alongside college classes or a full-time role?

Yes. The 40 classroom modules are self-paced, and the weekly mentor review session is scheduled so university students and working professionals can participate alongside their commitments.

Does the program involve live trading or stock recommendations?

No. In compliance with SEBI educational regulations, all analysis uses historical financial filings and delayed market data. We do not provide investment advice, stock tips, or live trading execution.
