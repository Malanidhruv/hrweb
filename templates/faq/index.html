Questions about our tools and training programs

Clear answers regarding the Market Analysis Learning Lab (Research Scanner), the Equity Research Internship Program, fees, certificates and regulatory compliance.

What is Harion Research?

Harion Research is a financial education and research platform based in Indore, India. We build public research tools—the Market Analysis Learning Lab (Research Scanner)—and offer structured analyst training through the Equity Research Internship Program.

Are the programs suitable for beginners?

Yes. The curriculum starts with financial statement and market structure foundations and progresses step by step to building a company model and writing a full equity research report. No prior trading or work experience is required.

What is the Market Analysis Learning Lab / Research Scanner?

It is our public web application (learninglab.streamlit.app) for studying NSE and BSE stock universes using delayed end-of-day price, volume, market-structure and fundamental financial ratio lenses. It is free to explore and serves as the starting tool in our internship workflow.

Are sessions live or recorded?

Both: you get 40 self-paced lessons in the Harion Classroom plus weekly live mentor sessions for interaction, doubt resolution and review of your financial model and draft report.

Do you use live market data or provide trading recommendations?

No. In line with SEBI educational guidelines, live discussions use historical market data and reported company filings. The Research Scanner runs on delayed End-of-Day data. We never provide investment advice, stock tips, target prices or trade execution.

Can I complete the internship alongside college or a full-time job?

Yes. The structure is designed to fit around university semesters or professional schedules.
