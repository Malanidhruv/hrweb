Report Standard

How Harion learning reports are structured

Every participant in the Equity Research Internship Program learns to write reports following a transparent five-part analytical structure:

SECTION 01

Business Overview

Revenue segments, products, customer base, unit economics and management track record derived from primary filings.

SECTION 02

Sector & Competitive Position

Industry structure, cyclicality, input-cost dynamics, regulation and peer-group comparisons.

SECTION 03

Financial Statement Analysis

Multi-year revenue, operating margins, ROCE/ROE decomposition, working-capital days, debt and cash-flow conversion.

SECTION 04

Valuation Context & Risks

Explicit cash-flow and peer-multiple assumptions paired with a thorough examination of what could go wrong.
